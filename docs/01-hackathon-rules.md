# Toggle Wars: Flagsmith hackathon brief

As of 25 Nov 2026. Sources: [overview](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/flagsmith), [rules](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/flagsmith/rules), [schedule](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/flagsmith/schedule), [resources](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/flagsmith/resources). Items marked **(unclear)** aren't stated on those pages.

## At a glance
- **What:** "Toggle Wars: The Flagsmith Experimentation Hackathon" by WeMakeDevs. Online and free.
- **Theme:** build something where Flagsmith feature flags do real work. Headline idea: put two versions behind a multivariate flag, choose a metric, split traffic, and let Flagsmith Experimentation (Bayesian A/B testing) pick the winner.
- **Hacking window:** **8:00 AM IST Thu 26 Nov to 8:00 PM IST Sun 29 Nov 2026** (02:30 UTC 26 Nov to 14:30 UTC 29 Nov). Late submissions are not accepted.
- **Prize pool:** advertised as "$5K": $2,000 + $1,000 + PS5/GTA 6 bundle + AirPods.

## Eligibility and teams
- Open worldwide. No age limit stated (unclear).
- Teams of 1 to 4. One team per person, one project per team.
- **Every team member registers individually** with a WeMakeDevs account. No registration deadline stated (unclear).
- Building with Flagsmith before the start is allowed. The only hard limit is the submission deadline.

## Submission requirements
1. A **public GitHub repository**.
2. A **README** with setup steps and **an explanation of how you used Flagsmith**.
3. A **short demo video** of the working project (judged on its own).

Unclear: how to submit (no form yet), max video length, license, whether deployment is required. Watch the WeMakeDevs and Flagsmith Discords.

## Tracks (enter ONE; judged only in that track; one winner per track)
| Track | Prize | What to build | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **01: Test Something Real** (ours) | $2,000 | An app running a real A/B test with Flagsmith Experimentation. The experiment matters more than app size. | 02: AI Agents and Feature Flags | $1,000 | Agent/tool using Flagsmith CLI, API or MCP. | Uses CLI/API/MCP, saves real time, human approves changes, clear output. |
| 03: Build on Open Source Flagsmith | PS5 + GTA 6 | Self-hosted Flagsmith + integration/dashboard/plugin/template. | Self-hosted, integrations, dev tools, reusable. |

**Bonus:** star [Flagsmith on GitHub](https://github.com/Flagsmith/flagsmith) for a random AirPods draw.

## Judging (no weights published)
1. **Use of Flagsmith:** core to the project; flags/experiments do real work.
2. **Impact:** solves a real problem developers would use.
3. **Creativity:** new idea, or familiar idea taken somewhere new.
4. **Demo video:** shows the problem, what you built, where Flagsmith fits.

Plus track criteria. Judges and results date not named. "Judging decisions are final."

## Disqualifiers
- Submitting after 8:00 PM IST, 29 Nov.
- Flagsmith not core.
- Plagiarism, harassment, tampering with judging ([Code of Conduct](https://www.wemakedevs.org/coc)).
- Breaking any rule on the rules page.
- **Not understanding your own code.** AI coding tools are allowed, but you must be able to explain what you submit.

Allowed: frameworks, OSS libraries, public APIs/datasets, AI assistants, building before the start. You keep ownership.
